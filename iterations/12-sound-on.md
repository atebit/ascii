# 12 · Sound and music on by default

- **Stage:** ship.
- **Asked:** music and sound should be on by default.
- **Changed:** sound starts on. Browsers block audio until a user gesture, so the first key, click or tap anywhere unlocks it and the music fades in (on the title, that's PRESS START). M or the HUD label still turns it off.
- **Check:** the music starts with the first press on a real device.
