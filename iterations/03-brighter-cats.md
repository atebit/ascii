# 03 · Brighter cats

- **Stage:** build, cat lab.
- **Asked:** the cats could be a little brighter.
- **Changed:** raised the fill and key lights (shared by both views). In the ASCII pass, a gentler brightness curve puts denser glyphs on the fur, the glyphs are brighter, and the cell fill went from 30% to 40%. Bright colors now scale down by their top channel instead of clipping, so pink stays pink rather than going white.
- **Check:** brighter without washing out the faces or the outlines.
