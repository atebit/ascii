# 08 · Darkwave music

- **Stage:** build, shell.
- **Asked:** a little darkwave song that sounds like it would be in Stranger Things, generated with JavaScript.
- **Changed:** [src/music.js](../src/music.js), an original loop generated live with Web Audio: D minor, 92 BPM, 32 bars (intro, build, full, breakdown) with arp, sub bass, pad, drums, a lead and echo. A stage change opens the filter for a moment. It starts and stops with the sound toggle ("[M] SOUND + MUSIC"), fades in over 2s and sits about 9 dB under the bleeps. It evokes the style without copying the show's theme.
- **Check:** how it actually sounds; nobody has heard it yet.
